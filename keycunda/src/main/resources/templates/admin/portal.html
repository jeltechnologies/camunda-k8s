Welcome to Keycunda

Keycunda is the OIDC identity provider for this Camunda 8 demo/learning environment. It replaces Keycloak with a small, purpose-built alternative for exactly what a demo box needs - nothing more.

Use the links in the top bar to get to:

  • Identity Management - human user accounts and the OAuth2 client-credentials applications ("Clients") external integrations authenticate as.
  • Secrets Management - an editable working copy of the connectors deployment's Kubernetes Secret, importable/exportable and applied to the cluster on demand.

Keycunda contains Identity Management and Kubernetes Secrets Management for Camunda 8 on a single Ubuntu box, for experimenting, learning and demos. Keycunda cannot be used in production, because this approach lacks the robustness, scale, high availability and license needed in enterprise environments.
